# Visual Try-On Tool Guidance

Use external apps as rough previews, not final truth. AI try-ons often ignore hair density, cowlicks, strand stiffness, salon execution, and daily styling difficulty.

## Current Tool Categories

- YouCam Makeup / YouCam AI Hairstyle: mature consumer app with many ratings. Often useful for quick visual preview. Free features may exist, but advanced AI hairstyles, high quality output, or more options may require subscription or in-app purchases.
- FaceApp: strong face editing and broad user base. Useful for rough style mood. Many advanced effects require Pro, and users should consider privacy before uploading personal photos.
- HaircutAI / hairstyle-ai websites: may provide focused hairstyle generation. Usually limited free previews or credits, then paid generation/download.

## Workflow With Visual Tools

1. Pick 2-3 candidate styles from the consultation, not random app presets.
2. Try them in an app using a clear front photo.
3. Bring back the generated previews.
4. Evaluate whether the shape flatters the face and whether the user's real hair can achieve it.
5. Convert the winning direction into salon instructions.

## Privacy Notes

Before uploading face photos, remind users to check privacy terms, retention policies, and whether the app trains on uploaded images. If privacy matters, prefer local editing tools or avoid uploading identifiable images.

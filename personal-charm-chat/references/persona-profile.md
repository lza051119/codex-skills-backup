# Personal Charm Profile

Build the profile as a practical texting configuration, not a personality essay.

## Minimum Questions

Ask only 3-6 questions at first:

1. What kind of person are you in real life? Pick 3-5 words.
2. What are your real hobbies, routines, or things you talk about with energy?
3. What do people usually appreciate about you?
4. What vibe do you want the other person to feel from you?
5. What do you hate sounding like in chat?
6. What is the current relationship stage and goal?

## Profile Output

Produce:

- **Core vibe**: one sentence.
- **Attraction assets**: 5-8 real strengths the user can show.
- **Topic anchors**: hobbies, routines, projects, places, opinions, small stories.
- **Natural proof**: concrete details that display value without bragging.
- **Humor style**: dry, silly, teasing, deadpan, warm, observational, self-deprecating, or low-humor.
- **Flirt ceiling**: none, light, medium, direct.
- **Reply length**: short, medium, or expressive.
- **Forbidden tone**: words, behaviors, and styles to avoid.
- **Lead style**: ask-led, story-led, playful-led, invite-led, opinion-led.

## Self-Display Principles

- Show through details, not claims. "I ran after work and got caught in the rain" is better than "I am disciplined."
- Use small stories. A 1-2 sentence slice of life is more attractive than a resume.
- Lead topic by bridging: receive their message, add one personal detail, then open a lane.
- Do not brag unless the context supports it. Prefer grounded confidence.
- Do not over-perform. The message should feel like a slightly better version of the user, not a new identity.

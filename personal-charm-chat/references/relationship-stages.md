# Relationship Stages

## Stage 1: Just Met

Goal: reduce awkwardness, find shared hooks, avoid heavy flirting.

Use: light curiosity, specific observations, simple self-disclosure.
Avoid: intense compliments, relationship talk, over-explaining.

## Stage 2: Friendly

Goal: build rhythm and recognizability.

Use: recurring jokes, small stories, taste, lifestyle details, mild teasing.
Avoid: being always available, interview mode, endless safe questions.

## Stage 3: Ambiguous

Goal: create tension while keeping comfort.

Use: playful framing, light compliments, callbacks, shared plans, mild challenge.
Avoid: confession dumps, jealousy games, sudden sexual pressure.

## Stage 4: After Date or Strong Interest

Goal: reward interest and create the next step.

Use: clear warmth, callback to a shared moment, specific next invitation.
Avoid: pretending not to care, over-testing, offering too many options.

## Stage 5: Cold or Awkward

Goal: preserve dignity and lower pressure.

Use: one light hook, one repair attempt if needed, then space.
Avoid: double/triple chasing, accusations, needy explanations.

## Escalation Check

Before making a reply warmer, check:

- Did she give a real opening, not just politeness?
- Is the topic light enough for playfulness?
- Has the user shown enough real self for flirting to feel grounded?
- Would a direct invite feel natural now, or should the chat build one more step?
- Is there any clear no, discomfort, or repeated low energy?

If the answer is uncertain, choose the lower-pressure version.
